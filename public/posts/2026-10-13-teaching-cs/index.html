Teaching CS at 7:30 AM

2026-10-13 · teaching · by Sho Byde

For a full school year I taught an intro computer science class at Granite Falls

High School - first period, 7:30 to 8:25, before clocking into my real job.

What I learned:

  • Nobody is too young or too rural to code. We got VS Code installed and the firewall configured; the rest was momentum.
  • Mistakes are the curriculum. I shared my screen while coding so students watched me break things and fix them in real time.
  • State machines explain everything. Bugs, loops, even why your game character walks through walls - draw the diagram and it clicks.
  • Refactoring isn't a chore - teaching it upfront changes how students write their first program, not their tenth.

If you're an engineer in a small town with a school nearby: do this. It costs

one early morning a week and it might matter more than any ticket you'll close.

Three of my students went on to take the advanced CS elective. Worth every 6 AM alarm.
